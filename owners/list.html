Owner terms

You run this place, or are allowed to act for whoever does, and it holds any council or other approvals it needs. What you send about the place (its facts, description, booking details and dates) is published in Open Camping DB under the Open Database Licence (ODbL 1.0), so anyone may use and share it. Your name and email stay private: we use them only to check your claim and contact you about this place. Facts we accept from you may also be offered to OpenStreetMap's volunteer mappers to check, without your name or email.

Open Camping DB is a free, open list of places to camp and stop in Australia. Data © OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Camping DB, under the ODbL.
