Open Camping DB for owners

Do you run a place to camp or stop?

Open Camping DB is a free, open list of campgrounds, rest areas and other stops in Australia, used by travel apps such as Example App. Owners can keep their place's details right, and owners of private land can list a farm stay.

  • Claim your placeIt is already in Open Camping DB and you run it.
  • List your private propertyGuests can stay on your land if they book with you first.
  • Update your placeYour claim is approved and you have your token.

How it works

  1. Send the claim or listing form. You get a short code and a token.
  2. Put the code on your own website or public Facebook page, so we can see the place is yours.
  3. A person checks your claim. Once it is approved, your place shows as owner verified.
  4. Use your token to update facts, your description, booking details or a temporary closure. Most changes appear within a day.

There is no account and no password. Keep your token safe: anyone who has it can change your place's details. If you lose it, claim your place again.
