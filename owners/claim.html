Owner terms

You run this place, or are allowed to act for whoever does, and it holds any council or other approvals it needs. What you send about the place (its facts, description, booking details and dates) is published in Open Camping DB under the Open Database Licence (ODbL 1.0), so anyone may use and share it. Your name and email stay private: we use them only to check your claim and contact you about this place. Read the full owner terms.
