Owner terms

These terms apply when you claim a place, list a private property or update a place in Open Camping DB as its owner.

When you tick the box, you agree that:

  1. You run the place, or you are allowed to act for whoever does.
  2. It has the approvals it needs. The place holds any council, planning or other approvals it needs to take guests, and you follow them. Open Camping DB does not check approvals.
  3. What you send is published openly. The facts, description, booking details and dates you send, and for a private property its name and location, become part of Open Camping DB. The database is published under the Open Database Licence (ODbL 1.0) and its contents under the Database Contents Licence (DbCL 1.0). Anyone may use, share and adapt them, and copies made before you change something keep the old version.
  4. You keep it accurate. You send only what is true and what you have the right to publish.
  5. Your name and email stay private. We use them only to check your claim and to contact you about this place. They are never published.
  6. A person checks every claim. We may refuse or remove a claim, a listing or any text at any time, for example when we cannot confirm that you run the place.
  7. Your token is like a key. Anyone who has it can change your place's details, so keep it private. If you lose it, claim the place again. The new claim replaces the old one once it is approved.
  8. Reviews belong to travellers. You cannot change or remove reviews, ratings or check-ins.

Questions, corrections and takedown requests: abuse@camporacle.com.au.
